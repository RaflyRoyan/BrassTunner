**Deskripsi lengkap:**

Brass Tuner adalah tuner khusus alat musik brass: trumpet, flugelhorn,
mellophone, horn F, alto horn, bariton, euphonium, trombon, dan tuba.

🎯 TUNER AKURAT
• Deteksi nada McLeod Pitch Method, akurat hingga ±2 cents dari 30 Hz
  (pedal tuba) sampai 1500 Hz (trumpet tinggi)
• Meter cents ±50 dengan warna hijau/kuning/merah
• Nada target: pilih nada, bunyikan, lalu lihat grafik input (Anda) vs
  output (target) secara langsung

🎺 KHAS BRASS

  dan posisi slide trombon
• Tips intonasi: mis. 1-3 dan 1-2-3 cenderung sharp → tarik slide 3
• Tabel penjarian lengkap per alat
• Mode lip slur: latih perpindahan partial dan cek ketepatan tiap nada

🎵 ALAT LATIHAN
• Drone / nada referensi (sinus atau gergaji)
• Metronom dengan tap tempo, bisa berjalan bersama tuner
• Multi birama: mis. 4/4 × 4 birama lalu 3/4 × 6 birama, berlanjut tanpa
  berhenti
• Mode nada panjang: grafik cents terhadap waktu

📊 RIWAYAT LATIHAN
• Sesi tersimpan otomatis
• Statistik nada yang paling sering sharp atau flat

🔒 PRIVAT & OFFLINE

## English

**App name:** Brass Tuner – Brass Instrument Tuner

**Short description:**
Accurate tuner for trumpet to tuba, with fingerings, drone and metronome.

**Full description:**

Brass Tuner is a tuner made for brass players: trumpet, flugelhorn,
mellophone, F horn, alto horn, baritone, euphonium, trombone and tuba.

🎯 ACCURATE TUNER
• McLeod Pitch Method detection, within ±2 cents from 30 Hz (tuba pedal
  tones) to 1500 Hz (high trumpet)
• ±50 cent meter with green/amber/red zones
• Target note: pick a note, play it, and watch a live graph of your
  input against the target output
• Written and concert pitch for B♭, F, E♭ and concert instruments
• A4 calibration from 430 to 450 Hz

🎺 MADE FOR BRASS
• Fingering for the note you play: 3 valves, 4 valves, F horn and
  trombone slide positions
• Intonation tips, e.g. 1-3 and 1-2-3 run sharp → pull the 3rd slide
• Full fingering chart per instrument
• Lip slur mode: practise partials and check every note

🎵 PRACTICE TOOLS
• Drone / reference tone (sine or saw)
• Metronome with tap tempo that runs alongside the tuner
• Multi meter: e.g. 4/4 for 4 bars, then 3/4 for 6 bars, without stopping
• Long tone mode: cents-over-time graph

📊 PRACTICE HISTORY
• Sessions saved automatically
• Stats on the notes you play sharp or flat most often

🔒 PRIVATE & OFFLINE
• 100% offline, no account, no ads
• Audio is processed on the device and never sent anywhere

Light and dark themes. Screen stays on while the tuner listens.
